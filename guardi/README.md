# Guardi 🛡️

**Know what's really in it.**

Take a picture of any product → AI aggregates cited third-party sources (EWG, CSPI,
FDA, USDA, peer-reviewed studies) → a plain-English readout of what's inside and how
it affects your health and environment → plus one cleaner swap, with small and
independent brands ranked first.

Guardi is an **aggregator with citations, not a judge of truth** — it reports what
sources say and links every claim.

## Business model (asset-light middleman)

1. **Affiliate** — commission on recommended swaps (no inventory, ever)
2. **Owned marketplace** — small clean-label brands sell directly, Guardi takes a cut
3. **Verified ads** — brands promote, but their claims get source-triangulated first
